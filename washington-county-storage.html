What does a 10×10 storage unit cost in Washington County?

Brickyard Road Storage lists a 10×10 standard-height unit for $80 per month at 1411 Brickyard Road in Chipley and $75 per month at 4269 Highway 77, its Wausau/Sunny Hills location. A 10×10 is about 100 square feet. Prices and availability can change, so confirm the current total with the company.

Before you sign

  • Check which location you are renting. The company shows $80/mo for Chipley and $75/mo for Wausau/Sunny Hills.
