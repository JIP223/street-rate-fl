Wind · insurance discounts for storm protection

Florida insurance discounts for storm protection.

These pages walk through the state wind-inspection form and how Florida insurance discounts for shutters, impact windows, and roof features work. Every fact has a date, and comes from an official source we opened. The discounts apply only to the windstorm coverage of the premium, not the whole policy. This is not insurance advice, and we don’t tell you what your premium will be.

The form · OIR-B1-1802

What the wind-inspection form records, and who is allowed to sign it

Checked November 19, 2026

Inspections on or after April 1, 2026 must use revision 04/26. A completed form can stay valid for up to five years if the house has not materially changed.

Discounts · Fla. Stat. § 627.711

How the insurance discount works, and why it is not 68% off the policy

Checked November 19, 2026

Discounts apply only to the windstorm coverage of the premium. The DFS guide’s 68% figure is a minimum on that windstorm portion, not 68% off the policy. Ask your insurer what it will apply to your policy.
