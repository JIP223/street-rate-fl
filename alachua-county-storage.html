Before you sign

  • Confirm the first-month offer. Three listings showed $1 for month one. The High Springs climate-controlled unit showed 50% off month one ($27).
  • Ask for the full move-in amount. All four listings show a one-time $29 admin fee. Confirm any tax, insurance, lock, or other charge.
  • Match the unit features to your needs. High Springs has an outside drive-up unit and a climate-controlled inside first-floor unit. Gainesville has a climate-controlled upstairs elevator unit at 8825 NW 13th Street and an outside drive-up unit at 6401 NW 120th Lane.
  • Compare online and in-store prices as separate channels. The page does not describe the in-store amount as a month-five rate.
