Before you sign

  • Confirm the first-month offer. The High Springs drive-up and Gainesville elevator units show $1 for month one; the High Springs climate-controlled unit shows $27 for month one.
  • Ask for the full move-in amount. Each listing shows a one-time $29 admin fee. Confirm any tax, insurance, lock, or other charge.
  • Match the unit features to your needs. High Springs has a drive-up outside unit and a climate-controlled first-floor unit. Gainesville’s listing is climate-controlled upstairs with elevator access.
  • Compare online and in-store prices as separate channels. The page does not describe the in-store amount as a month-five rate.
