What would a year cost?

The listings give no full-year price for an available unit. Ask how long the starting rate lasts and what the company can charge later.

Before you sign

  • Contact the Bonifay location before paying. Tharp & Sons asks renters to check that a unit is open on site. Its separate Chipley and DeFuniak Springs listings cover other counties.
  • Ask for the total due at move-in, including any lock, coverage, tax or deposit. Keep the quote with the unit number and date.
