What this means for you

  • Fanning Springs sits on the Gilchrist County line. The listing’s address, 16910 NW US Highway 19, follows Levy County’s numbering on that road.
  • Local companies may still rent 10×10 units here. Most small companies quote by phone instead of posting a price, and we don’t list a number we couldn’t see on a page.
  • At Public Storage, the $1 offer covers the first month only. Ask for the total due at move-in, with the $29 fee, and the rent from month two.
