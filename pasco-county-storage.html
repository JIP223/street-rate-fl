Before you sign

  • Ask for the price after any special ends. Confirm the exact monthly rent and when it starts.
  • Ask for every one-time fee in dollars. The listing showed a $29 admin fee.
  • Check the unit features. Climate control, floor, elevator and drive-up access affect the price.
  • Confirm the full move-in cost. Ask whether insurance, taxes or other charges apply.
  • Read the lease for future rent changes. A displayed rate does not guarantee the rent stays fixed.
