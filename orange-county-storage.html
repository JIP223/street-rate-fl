Before you sign

  • Ask for the price after month four in writing. Both Public Storage pages showed a different in-store rent for months 5–12.
  • Ask for the admin fee in dollars. The Public Storage listings showed a one-time $29 fee.
  • Check the unit features. The drive-up unit and climate-controlled elevator-access unit had different rates.
  • Confirm the full move-in cost. Extra Space says taxes, fees and insurance may apply; read the rental agreement before booking.
  • Read the lease for future rent changes. A listed later rate does not guarantee that rent stays fixed after month 12.
