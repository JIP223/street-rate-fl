Before you sign

  • Ask what the rent is after the first month. Outside the two four-month offers, the online rent is $10 to $30 a month less than the in-store rent the same page displays.
  • Two locations sell the first four months at half price, then the months 5–12 rent: 3009 53rd Ave E and 2100 US Highway 301 N.
  • 2900 US 301 N in Ellenton lists half off the first month, not $1: $38, then $76 online.
  • Ask for the total due at move-in, including the $29 admin fee, a lock, coverage and tax.
  • Compare access. Upstairs and elevator units here are cheaper than drive-up units of the same size.
