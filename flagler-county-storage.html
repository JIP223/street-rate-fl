What does 10×10 storage cost in Flagler County?

CubeSmart shows four priced 10×10 units in Flagler County, split across two Palm Coast locations. All four have a first month free and 40% off offer; the online rent runs from $61.80 to $105.60 a month against in-store rents of $103 to $176. Public Storage has no Flagler County location page, so CubeSmart is the only company in this county check. Every amount below was on the company’s own page on November 21, 2026.

Before you sign

  • Ask what the rent is after the offer. CubeSmart's in-store rents here are $41 to $70 a month higher than the online rents.
  • The offer is shown as First Month Free plus 40% off. Ask whether the 40% applies to every month of the rental or only to the promotion.
  • No admin fee amount is shown on these listings; ask for the move-in total in writing.
  • Two locations serve Palm Coast: 531 Cypress Edge Dr and 11 Pine Lakes Pkwy N. Compare both before booking.
  • Confirm the unit type. The drive-up unit at 11 Pine Lakes Pkwy N is the cheapest 10×10 we checked.
