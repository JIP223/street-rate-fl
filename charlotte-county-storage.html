What does 10×10 storage cost in Charlotte County?

Public Storage shows four priced 10×10 units in Charlotte County, at two Port Charlotte locations and two in Punta Gorda. Three list $1 for the first month, then an online rent from $36 to $61 a month; 6221 Marlympia Way lists a half-price first month instead, $39, then $78. Before you sign

  • Ask what the rent is after the first month. The online rents here are $11 to $19 a month below the in-store rents on the same pages.
  • 6221 Marlympia Way lists half off the first month, $39, not $1.
  • The company calls 10500 Winborough Drive a climate-controlled locker, so it is not counted as a 10×10 room on this page.
  • Ask for the total due at move-in, including the $29 admin fee, a lock, coverage and tax.
  • Check the access type before booking: 12905 Gulfstream Blvd is an upstairs elevator unit and the cheapest here.
