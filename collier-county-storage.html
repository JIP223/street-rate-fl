Before you sign

  • Ask what the rent is after the offer. Naples online rents here run $14 to $27 a month below the in-store rent on the same page.
  • 3555 Radio Road and 15800 Old 41 North sell months 1–4 at half price, then the months 5–12 rent.
  • 3807 White Lake Blvd shows no first-month offer, so the year table charges the online rent for all 12 months.
  • Ask for the total due at move-in, including the $29 admin fee, a lock, coverage and tax.
  • Compare access. 1361 Airport Rd S and 13191 Tamiami Trl E are elevator units; the cheaper drive-up units sit further east.
