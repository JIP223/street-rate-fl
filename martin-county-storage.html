Before you sign

  • The Public Storage ground-floor unit near a door or elevator has a shorter first-period discount than the other two units at that address. Ask which promotion applies.
  • For the CubeSmart unit, ask whether the free first month and the 40% offer apply to the online price, and what you would pay after the first month.
  • Ask for the total due at move-in, including any lock, coverage, tax or deposit. Keep the quote with the unit number and date.
