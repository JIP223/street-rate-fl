Before you sign

  • Ask when the Extra Space flash sale ends. Its first-floor climate-controlled 10×10 is marked “Flash Sale,” but the page does not give a duration.
  • Compare the unit type. Extra Space lists a climate-controlled first-floor option and a separate drive-up 10×10 at different prices.
  • Ask for CubeSmart’s administrative fee in dollars. The page says a one-time fee is due with the first month but does not show the amount.
  • Confirm insurance coverage. CubeSmart says stored items must be insured; ask whether your existing policy qualifies.
  • Get the full move-in total in writing. Ask both companies about taxes, insurance, administrative fees, and any other charges before booking.
