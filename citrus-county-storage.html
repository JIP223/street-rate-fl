Before you sign

  • Confirm the unit and price. Features like climate control and access type can change the amount.
  • Ask about fees. These pages did not show a one-time administrative fee; confirm any fees before renting.
  • Ask when an introductory rate changes. Tellus describes its web price as an introductory rate that may change after move-in; check the rental agreement.
  • Check availability and payment terms. AA Storage lists different cash/check and card amounts.
