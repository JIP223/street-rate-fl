Before you sign

  • Ask how long the offer lasts. Confirm the regular monthly price after a special ends.
  • Ask for one-time fees in dollars. The page showed a $29 admin fee.
  • Check the unit features. Climate control, floor, elevator and drive-up access affect the price.
  • Confirm the full move-in cost. Ask whether insurance, taxes or other charges apply.
  • Read the lease for future rent changes. A listed monthly rate does not guarantee it stays fixed.
