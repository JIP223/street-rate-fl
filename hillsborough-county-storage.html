What does a 10×10 storage unit cost in Hillsborough County?

A 10×10 unit has about 100 square feet. We checked four Tampa listings from two storage companies. Public Storage shows a lower price for the first four months and a higher rent for months 5–12. CubeSmart lists online and in-store prices, but does not give a month-by-month schedule on the page we checked.

Before you sign

  • Ask what happens after month 4. The two Public Storage pages show different rents for months 5–12.
  • Confirm the $29 admin fee. It is listed for both Public Storage units; ask for the full move-in amount.
  • Compare the same CubeSmart unit online and in-store. The listed prices differ by booking channel, not by month.
  • Ask about other required charges. Confirm insurance, taxes, lock costs, and the full lease terms before paying.
