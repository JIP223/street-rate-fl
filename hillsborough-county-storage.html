Hillsborough County 10×10 storage: the ad price vs month five.

A 10×10 fits about a one-bedroom apartment. In Hillsborough County the ad price is usually a four-month starter price, and from month 5 the same unit lists for about twice as much. Below is every listing we found that shows both numbers, from Tampa to Plant City — plus the listings that show an online price next to a higher in-store price.

Before you sign

  • Ask for the month-5 price in writing. On the 50% off listings above, the price about doubles from month 5.
  • Compare the month-5 price, not the ad. At 13611 N 15th St in Tampa the ad is $36, the lowest on this page, and month 5 is $72 — also the lowest. At 1302 W Kennedy Blvd the ad is $70 and month 5 is $140, the highest later price here.
  • Ask for the admin fee in dollars. Neither company showed an amount on the pages we read.
  • Ask how long the online price lasts. CubeSmart showed a discounted online price next to a higher in-store price, but no end date for the discount — and its terms say the rate can change with 30 days’ notice.
  • A $1 first month only covers month 1. The monthly rent after that is the number that matters — at 2504 State Rd 60 in Valrico, that is $119 a month online.
  • If you live in Town ’n’ Country or Carrollwood, search Tampa. That is how both companies list the locations near you.
