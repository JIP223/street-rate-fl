Before you sign

  • Ask how long the ad price lasts. Public Storage showed four-month discounts beside the listed later rent. CubeSmart showed online and in-store prices but did not say when the higher figure applies.
  • Ask for every one-time fee in dollars. Public Storage showed a $29 admin fee. CubeSmart says its admin fee is due with the first month’s payment but does not show the amount on these pages.
  • Confirm the unit features. Drive-up, first-floor and elevator-access units have different prices in the listings above.
  • Price insurance before booking. CubeSmart requires insurance coverage. Ask whether your existing renter’s or homeowner’s policy meets its requirements.
  • Read the lease for future increases. A web page’s later or in-store rate does not lock the rate for the full year.
