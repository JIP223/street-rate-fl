Each listing also shows a one-time $29 admin fee. A Value Store It page lists 10×10 online prices separately; it does not give a month-by-month rate schedule.

Before you sign

  • Ask for the rent after month four in writing.
  • Ask whether the Value Store It free-month offer has additional conditions.
  • Ask for the full move-in amount, including admin fee, taxes, lock, and insurance.
  • Confirm the exact floor, unit location, access, and availability in the lease.
