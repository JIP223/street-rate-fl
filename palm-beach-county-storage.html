Palm Beach County 10×10 storage: the ad price vs month five.

A 10×10 fits about a one-bedroom apartment. In Palm Beach County the ad price is usually a four-month starter price, and from month 5 the same unit lists for 40% to 100% more. Below is every listing we found that shows both numbers, from Jupiter to Boca Raton — plus the listings that show an online price next to a higher in-store price.

Before you sign

  • Ask for the month-5 price in writing. On the 50% off listings above, the price about doubles from month 5.
  • Compare the month-5 price, not the ad. At 400 E Industrial Ave in Boynton Beach the ad is $99, higher than most, but month 5 is $141 — and the jump is only $42, the smallest on this page.
  • Ask for the admin fee in dollars. Public Storage’s fine print says an administrative fee applies, but no listing we read showed an amount.
  • Ask how long the online price lasts. CubeSmart showed a discounted online price next to a higher in-store price, but no end date for the discount.
  • A $1 first month or a free first month only covers month 1. The monthly rent after that is the number that matters — at 3111 Fortune Way in Wellington, that is $206 to $219 a month online.
  • Promos are for new customers only, on selected units, subject to availability. Public Storage says a reservation is required to guarantee the price.
  • Ask about insurance. The listings did not show a price for it.
