Before you sign

  • Ask for the month-5 price in writing. On the 50% off listings above, the price about doubles from month 5.
  • Compare annual costs for the same unit size. The two $125 introductory offers rise to $250 in month five. At either location that adds $1,000 over the remaining eight months.
  • Include the $29 fee when comparing Public Storage offers. Confirm any insurance or taxes separately.
  • Ask how long the online price lasts. CubeSmart showed a discounted online price next to a higher in-store price, but no end date for the discount.
  • A $1 first month or a free first month only covers month 1. The monthly rent after that is the number that matters — at 3111 Fortune Way in Wellington, that is $204 to $210 a month online.
  • Promos are for new customers only, on selected units, subject to availability. Public Storage says a reservation is required to guarantee the price.
  • Ask about insurance. The listings did not show a price for it.
