Before you sign

  • Confirm the first-month offer. Both 10×10 units show $1 for month one; the listed online rent is $51 or $53 per month afterward.

  • Choose the right features. One unit is outside with drive-up access; the other is inside, climate-controlled, and on the first floor.
  • Compare the booking channels. The $64 and $66 in-store amounts are separate from monthly online rent.
