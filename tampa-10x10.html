Tampa 10×10 storage: advertised vs typical rate after 90 days

A 10×10 is the usual “one-bedroom apartment” size. The number in the ad is often a short promo. Below are rates we copied from operator pages on November 18, 2026, America/New_York. We only show a later rate when the operator published one.

Sources: Public Storage Tampa 10×10 listing and CubeSmart, 12690 Race Track Rd, Tampa, FL 33626. Extra Space official facility pages returned an access error from our fetch, so Extra Space is omitted rather than guessed. Affiliate link — not yet active

How to read “after 90 days.” Public Storage labeled some Tampa units “Month 1–4 50% off” and, next to that, “Months 5–12 in-store rent.” Month 5 is when a four-month promo ends (about 120 days, not exactly 90). We use that published months 5–12 figure as the after-promo number. It is not a guarantee your bill will be that amount — confirm in writing. CubeSmart showed an online price and a higher in-store price, plus “First Month Free & 40% Off,” but did not say how many months the 40% off lasts, so we do not project a CubeSmart total after 90 days.

Promo, insurance, and admin-fee traps

  • The headline rent is often a short intro. Ask what the rate becomes in month 2 and in month 5, in writing.
  • Admin fees are easy to miss. We saw a $29 one-time fee on some Public Storage Tampa listings, and CubeSmart requires an administrative fee without publishing the amount on that facility page.
  • Insurance is often required and rarely priced on the listing. CubeSmart says coverage is required. We did not find a Public Storage insurance dollar amount on the Tampa 10×10 listing we used. Budget for a monthly protection plan unless you can show a renter’s or homeowners policy they accept.
  • Online-only prices may not be the in-store price. Walking in can mean the higher number.
  • Rates can still change after the promo with notice under a typical month-to-month lease. A published months 5–12 rate is not a locked 12-month contract unless the lease says so.
