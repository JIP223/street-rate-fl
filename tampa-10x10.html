Before you sign

  • Ask for the month-2 and month-5 rate in writing. The headline rent is often a short intro.
  • Ask for the admin fee in dollars. We saw $29 on some Public Storage listings; CubeSmart requires a fee without publishing it.
  • Price the insurance. CubeSmart requires coverage. We found no Public Storage insurance amount on the listing we used. A renter’s or homeowners policy may be accepted instead.
  • Check online vs in-store. Walking in can mean the higher number.
  • Read the lease for rate changes. A published months 5–12 rate is not a locked 12-month contract unless the lease says so.
