What does 10×10 storage cost in Brevard County?

CubeSmart adds six priced 10×10s in Merritt Island, Palm Bay and Rockledge, from $67.20 a month online against an in-store price of $112. Before you sign

  • Ask what the rent is after the offer. Public Storage lists 50% off for months 1–4 at seven Brevard locations, then the months 5–12 rent, which is also its in-store rate.
  • Compare the booking channel. Where Public Storage shows both, the online monthly rent is lower than the in-store rent.
  • CubeSmart's online price is its 25%, 40% or 50% offer. Ask what the rent becomes when the offer ends; the company does not say on these pages.
  • Ask for the total due at move-in, including the $29 Public Storage admin fee, a lock, insurance or tax.
  • Check the access type. Upstairs and elevator units at 4345 South St and 4630 Babcock St NE cost less than drive-up units here.
