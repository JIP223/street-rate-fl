Privacy

How we handle visits to this site.

Street Rate FL is made of fixed web pages. We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read. Google sets cookies for that. We don’t sell data or run ads.

This policy is dated November 19, 2026.

What this site is

These pages are simple files hosted on GitHub Pages. There are no accounts, comments, newsletters, or forms. The site itself does not ask you for a name, email address, or any other personal data.

Cookies, analytics, and tracking

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read. Google sets cookies for that. We don’t sell that data, and we don’t run ads. See Google’s privacy policy for how Google handles the data. The one small script on our price pages only turns the check date into “checked N days ago” in your browser. It sends nothing anywhere.

What GitHub may record

GitHub, the company that hosts GitHub Pages, may record technical information when you visit, including your IP address. That is GitHub’s doing, not a script we added. See GitHub’s privacy statement for how it handles that data.

Search Console

We use Google Search Console to see overall information about searches that show this site, like how often it appears and which searches it appears for. Google does not give us a personal profile of individual visitors.

If you email us

If you write to us from the contact page, we receive your email address and what you wrote. We use it only to answer you and to fix the page you wrote about. We do not add you to any list.

Partner and affiliate links

We don’t currently have active booking or affiliate links. If we add them later, they will be marked. Clicking a marked partner link takes you to that partner’s site, which may set its own cookies and collect data under its own policy. That would start only after you click.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
