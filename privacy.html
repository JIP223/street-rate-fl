Privacy

We don’t collect anything about you.

Street Rate FL is a static website. We do not collect personal information from visitors, and we do not set cookies or run analytics or tracking scripts on this site.

This policy is dated November 19, 2026.

What this site is

These pages are static HTML files hosted on GitHub Pages. There are no accounts, comments, newsletters, or forms, and the site itself does not ask you for a name, email address, or any other personal data.

Cookies, analytics, and tracking

This site does not set cookies. It does not include analytics, advertising, or other tracking scripts. The one small script on our price pages only turns the check date into “checked N days ago” in your browser; it sends nothing anywhere.

What the host may log

GitHub, as the company that hosts GitHub Pages, may log technical information when you visit, including your IP address. That is GitHub’s doing, not a script we added. See GitHub’s privacy statement for how it handles that data.

Search Console

We use Google Search Console to see aggregate information about searches that show this site, such as how often it appears and which queries it appears for. Google does not give us a personal profile of individual visitors.

If you email us

If you write to us from the contact page, we receive your email address and what you wrote. We use it only to answer you and to fix the page you wrote about, and we do not add you to any list.

Partner and affiliate links

We do not currently have active partner or affiliate links. If we add them later, they will be marked. Clicking a marked partner link takes you to that partner’s site, which may set its own cookies and collect data under its own policy. That would start only after you click.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
