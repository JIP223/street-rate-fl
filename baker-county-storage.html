Before you sign

  • Ask whether the listed rent is fixed or can change during your rental.
  • Budget for the $19.99 admin fee and $25 non-refundable deposit.
  • The company says its rates are plus tax and insurance coverage, and autopay is required; ask for the full move-in amount.
  • Ask All Safe Mini Storage for a written 10×10 quote because its unit page does not show prices.
