Baker County · 10×10 units

What does a 10×10 storage unit cost in Baker County?

Storage Space Inc. in Macclenny lists a 10×10 non-climate-controlled unit at $124.99 per month and a climate-controlled 10×10 at $199.99 per month. Its page also lists a $19.99 one-time admin fee and a $25 non-refundable deposit. All Safe Mini Storage lists 10×10 unit types but does not show their prices online.

First-year cost

The company does not show whether either rent amount stays fixed for 12 months, so we are not projecting a yearly total. Ask what the rent will be after move-in and include the published admin fee, deposit, tax, and insurance in your budget.

Before you sign

  • Ask whether the monthly rent can change and when.
  • Include the $19.99 admin fee and $25 deposit, plus tax and insurance.
  • Confirm which unit is climate-controlled and whether autopay is required.
  • Ask All Safe Mini Storage for its current 10×10 price; its website does not show one.
