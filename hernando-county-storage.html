Before you sign

  • Ask what the rent is after the first month. At seven locations the online rent is $14 to $28 a month below the in-store rent shown on the same page.
  • 4080 Mariner Blvd sells months 1–4 at half price, then $121 a month for months 5–12.
  • Ask for the total due at move-in, including the $29 admin fee, a lock, coverage and tax.
  • Compare access. The elevator unit at 10437 County Line Rd is $49 online; drive-up units on Commercial Way are $66 to $76.
  • 7294 Broad St in Brooksville is the priciest 10×10 here at $114 online; check whether a Spring Hill location is closer before paying more.
