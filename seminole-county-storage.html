Before you sign

  • Two indoor ground-floor roll-up 10×10s at 510 Douglas Avenue have the same later rent of $110, but different first-four-month prices ($55 and $77). Ask which promotion applies to the unit you would get.
  • Ask for the months 5–12 figure in writing, and for the total due at move-in, including any lock, coverage, tax or deposit. Keep the quote with the unit number and date.
