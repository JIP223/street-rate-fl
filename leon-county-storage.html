What does 10×10 storage cost in Leon County?

Three companies post 10×10 prices in Leon County, all in Tallahassee. Public Storage lists $1 first months at two locations, then online rents of $76 and $92 a month against in-store rents of $95 and $115. CubeSmart lists a first month free plus 40% off at two locations, $61.20 to $118.80 a month online against $102 to $198 in store. Extra Space advertises 10×10 prices from $53 to $67 a month and does not show a later rate. Every amount below was shown on the company's own page on November 21, 2026.

Before you sign

  • Ask what the rent is after the offer. CubeSmart's in-store rents at these two locations are $41 to $79 a month above its online rents.
  • Extra Space shows one advertised 10×10 price per location and no later rate, so its first-year total is not calculable from the page; call for the term.
  • Public Storage charges a $29 admin fee on both Tallahassee listings.
  • Ask whether the CubeSmart 40% applies to every month or only to the offer month.
  • Compare access: the cheapest CubeSmart unit is an elevator unit at 3200 W Tennessee St, and the most expensive is a first-floor unit on Capital Circle NE.
