Before you sign

  • Ask for the month-5 price in writing. On the 50% off listings above, the price about doubles from month 5.
  • Compare the month-5 price, not the ad. At 7200 W 20th Ave in Hialeah the ad is $113, higher than most, but month 5 is $161, lower than most.
  • Ask for the admin fee in dollars. We saw a one-time $29 fee on some listings, including 7511 NW 73rd Street and 10505 Marlin Road. Others did not show an amount.
  • Ask about insurance. The listings did not show a price for it. A renter’s or homeowners policy may be accepted instead.
  • Check online vs in-store. Every listing showed a lower online price than the in-store price.
  • Month to month means the price can change. Public Storage says its rentals are month to month, so a months 5–12 price on the website is not a locked contract unless your lease says so.
