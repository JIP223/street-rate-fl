What does a 10×10 storage unit cost in Highlands County?

AA Storage lists 10×10×9 non-climate-controlled units at both Sebring locations: $85 per month by cash or check, or $88.39 by card, including a small processing fee. A 10×10 has about 100 square feet. These are payment-method prices, not a rent increase over time.

Before you sign

  • Choose cash/check or card. The listed card price includes a processing fee.
  • Check the contract terms. AA Storage says rents are non-refundable, including annual contracts; it advises choosing a year-long option only if you expect to stay at least a year.
  • Confirm climate and unit dimensions. These listings are non-climate-controlled, and the company says ceiling heights are approximate.
  • Confirm availability and the full total. No separate admin fee is shown on the pricing page.
