Before you sign

  • Ask whether the listed rate is introductory and when it changes.
  • Ask for the full move-in amount, including any one-time fee, tax, lock, and insurance.
  • Confirm the exact unit size, floor, climate control, and access details on your lease.
  • Save a copy of the quote and ask how much notice is required for a rent change.
