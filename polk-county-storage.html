What does 10×10 storage cost in Polk County?

Two companies show priced 10×10 units in Polk County: Public Storage in Winter Haven, Auburndale and Davenport, and CubeSmart in Lakeland. Public Storage lists $1 for the first month, then online monthly rent from $58 to $156. CubeSmart lists a first month free and online rent from $67.80 to $114.60, against in-store prices up to $191. Before you sign

  • Ask how long the online rent lasts. Public Storage shows a first-month offer and a monthly web rent, but no end date for that rent.
  • Compare the two booking channels. Public Storage lists an online rent and a higher in-store rent at every location above.
  • On CubeSmart, the online price is shown with a First Month Free & 40% Off offer; ask whether the 40% applies to every month or only to the offer.
  • Ask for the total due at move-in, including the $29 Public Storage admin fee, a lock, insurance or tax.
  • Match the access type to your move. Upstairs and elevator units cost less than drive-up units in this sample.
