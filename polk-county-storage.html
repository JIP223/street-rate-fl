What does a 10×10 storage unit cost in Polk County?

In Lakeland, CubeSmart shows 10×10 monthly online prices from $75 to $160.20, with in-store prices from $125 to $267 at one location. Extra Space Storage’s Lakeland page shows an area-wide 10×10 starting price of $38 and an average of $79. Those area figures are not a quote for a specific unit.

Before you sign

  • Ask what online rate will appear on your lease and when it can change.
  • Ask for the full move-in cost, including fees, taxes, insurance, and any lock.
  • Compare units with the same floor, access, and climate-control features.
  • Confirm availability directly; area averages do not guarantee a unit at that price.
