About & methodology

Street Rate FL is an independent directory. We are not affiliated with Public Storage, Extra Space, CubeSmart, or any other operator.

How prices are gathered

  • We read the operator’s own public web page for a named facility or city listing.
  • We record the advertised / online price, and only record an “after promo” or “typical later” price when the operator publishes one (for example, “Months 5–12 in-store rent”).
  • We note one-time admin fees and insurance rules only when that same page states them. If a dollar amount is not published, we say so.
  • Every figure is dated. The check date on current pages is November 18, 2026, America/New_York.
  • If we cannot open or verify a page, we leave that operator out rather than guess. Extra Space facility pages blocked our fetch on the first Tampa page, so Extra Space prices are not listed there.

What this is not

These are not quotes, promises, or averages for the whole market. Online rates change, often daily. In-store prices and later “market adjustments” can differ from what we saw. Confirm the month-one total and the rate after any promo in writing before you pay.

Affiliate disclosure

If we later join a partner program (for example SpareFoot, Storage.com, or Neighbor), we may earn a commission when you reserve through a marked link. That would not change the prices we transcribe from operator pages.
