How we check prices

How we check prices.

Street Rate FL is an independent site. We are not tied to Public Storage, Extra Space, CubeSmart, or any other storage company, and no storage company pays us.

Who runs Street Rate FL

A small independent team that lists storage prices for people renting in Florida. We don’t own or run storage facilities, and we don’t take money from storage companies. Reach us through the contact page.

How we get the prices

  • We read the storage company’s own public web page for a named location or city.
  • We write down the ad or online price. We add a later price only when the company shows one, for example “Months 5–12 in-store rent.”
  • We only note a one-time admin fee or an insurance rule when that same page states it. If a dollar amount isn’t shown, we say so.
  • Every number has a date. The check date on current pages is November 18, 2026, America/New_York.
  • If we can’t open or confirm a page, we leave that company out instead of guessing. Extra Space location pages blocked our check on the first Tampa page, so Extra Space prices are not listed there.

How we check wind pages

The hurricane and wind insurance discount pages use official sources only: Florida statutes, Department of Financial Services guides and forms pages, and Office of Insurance Regulation forms. Every fact has a date. We don’t invent quotes, prices, or discounts, and we leave a figure out if we can’t point to a source. Those pages are not insurance advice. They point you to the official form, and to your insurer for any amount that would apply to your policy.

What this is not

These are not quotes, promises, or averages for the whole market. Online prices change, often daily. The price in the office, or later on, can differ from what we saw. Before you pay, get the first-month total and the price after any deal in writing.

Corrections

If a price on this site is wrong or out of date, tell us which page and what you saw. We go back to the company’s website, update the number and the check date at the top of the page, and say on the page what changed. We don’t change a number just because someone asks. Only the company’s own page decides it.

Affiliate disclosure

If we later join a partner program (for example SpareFoot, Storage.com, or Neighbor), we may earn a fee when you book through a marked link. That would not change the prices we take from the companies’ websites.
