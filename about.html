Method and disclosure

How we check prices.

Street Rate FL is an independent directory. We are not affiliated with Public Storage, Extra Space, CubeSmart, or any other operator, and no operator pays us.

Who runs Street Rate FL

A small independent team that publishes self-storage prices for Florida renters. We do not own or manage storage facilities and do not take payments from operators. Reach us through the contact page.

How prices are gathered

  • We read the operator’s own public web page for a named facility or city listing.
  • We record the advertised or online price, and record a later price only when the operator publishes one (for example, “Months 5–12 in-store rent”).
  • We note one-time admin fees and insurance rules only when that same page states them. If a dollar amount is not published, we say so.
  • Every figure is dated. The check date on current pages is November 18, 2026, America/New_York.
  • If we cannot open or verify a page, we leave that operator out rather than guess. Extra Space facility pages blocked our check on the first Tampa page, so Extra Space prices are not listed there.

What this is not

These are not quotes, promises, or averages for the whole market. Online rates change, often daily. In-store prices and later rate changes can differ from what we saw. Confirm the month-one total and the rate after any promo in writing before you pay.

Corrections

If a price on this site is wrong or out of date, tell us which page and what you saw. We re-check the operator’s page, update the number and the check date at the top of the page, and say on the page what changed. We do not change a number because someone asks; only the operator’s published page decides it.

Affiliate disclosure

If we later join a partner program (for example SpareFoot, Storage.com, or Neighbor), we may earn a commission when you reserve through a marked link. That would not change the prices we copy from operator pages.
