Before you sign

  • Ask when the online rate ends. CubeSmart’s page does not show a month-by-month schedule for the units above.
  • Get every fee in dollars. Public Storage lists a $29 admin fee here. CubeSmart requires a one-time fee but does not show the amount on its page.
  • Ask about insurance. CubeSmart requires coverage and says it accepts proof of your own policy.
  • Confirm the unit details and availability. Floor, drive-up access, climate control, and inventory differ by listing.
  • Keep the quote. Ask how rent can change during a month-to-month lease.
