Before you sign

  • At 6665 Wiley Road, the card shows $86 for months 1–4 and $123 from month 5, but the same page also says “Enjoy the same monthly rate for your first year.” Ask which one applies before you book.
  • At 3211 San Pablo Rd S, the two climate-controlled 10×10s differ by $15 a month online ($133 with elevator access, $148 on the ground floor), and only the ground-floor one shows 50% off month one instead of $1.
  • On the $1-first-month listings, ask whether the online rent is locked for months 2–12 or can change, and what you'd pay in-store instead.
  • Ask for the full move-in cost, including the $29 admin fee, lock, taxes and insurance.
